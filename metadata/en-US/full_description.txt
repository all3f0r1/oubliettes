Oubliettes is a logic puzzle. Each grid hides a dungeon: work out where every wall is from the clues, with no guessing needed. Every grid has exactly one solution.

<b>Rules</b>
<ul>
<li>The numbers along the edge give the number of walls in each row and column.</li>
<li>Monsters and chests are never walls.</li>
<li>Every monster sits in a dead end, and every dead end holds a monster.</li>
<li>Every chest is in a treasure room: 3×3 open cells, one chest, one opening.</li>
<li>Elsewhere, hallways are one cell wide and the whole dungeon is connected.</li>
</ul>

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Campaign: 250 levels that are the same for everyone, in five difficulties (6×6 to 14×14), all open from the start.</li>
<li>Endless: randomly generated grids in the difficulty you pick.</li>
<li>Tutorial: a dungeon you solve step by step, one rule at a time.</li>
<li>Drag to fill a row or column, undo and redo, progress and history saved after every move.</li>
<li>Screen reader support, a magnifier for large grids, plain digits and a calm mode without animation.</li>
<li>Endless grids are generated on the device. No ads, no tracking, no account.</li>
<li>Original medieval music loops and sound effects, with volume sliders.</li>
</ul>

The music can optionally be replaced by the live stream of Ancient FM (ancientfm.com). This is off by default and is the only thing the app uses the network for.
